About Histography

Histography is an atlas you explore one mystery at a time. Each mystery is a place on Earth where something happened: a city, a ruin, a river, a discovery, a life.

You begin with a single difficult clue. Ask for more when you need them; each of the five is easier than the last. When you think you know, turn the planet until the place sits under the centre mark and confirm it.

The mysteries reach from the first Bronze Age cities to the present day, across every continent. There are no scores and no clock. The reward is the story behind the place.

Developer tools

Drag to turn the Earth, scroll to zoom. Keyboard: arrows turn, Q and E roll, N restores north, plus and minus zoom.
