# Game vision

Globe is a modern successor to Topocopter with no helicopter. The player directly manipulates a planet Earth: rotate it beneath the camera, zoom continuously, and find countries, cities, and other features.

The intended experience combines a beautiful realistic Earth with clear educational feedback. Future rounds will present destinations, score geographical accuracy, and optionally measure time. Challenge difficulty, scoring thresholds, accessibility options, and content coverage remain design work, not established gameplay rules.

Long-term visuals include terrain, satellite imagery, oceans, atmosphere, and progressive detail. Political borders, labels, and teaching overlays remain separate switchable layers. Accuracy takes priority over fabricated detail. Sources, uncertainty, contested borders, and place-name policies need explicit treatment before public educational content ships.

The initial title offers a short vision panel only. It makes no claims about available Earth exploration or challenges. The abstract orbital linework is not geographical data.

Acceptance for the first playable milestone: a coordinated Earth asset, tested coordinate mapping, smooth direct rotation/zoom, and a small reviewed destination set. Coordinate with Claude before this work begins.
