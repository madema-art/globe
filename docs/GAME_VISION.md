# Game vision

Globe teaches world geography and world history through curiosity, exploration, and five-clue mysteries. Inspired by Topocopter, it uses direct interaction with a 3D planet Earth and no helicopter. Historical coverage should eventually span the Bronze Age to the present and cultures across the whole world.

The owner has established Education Mode as the sole current mode. There are no scores, timers, formal rounds, competitive elements, achievements, difficulty settings, or predetermined progression system. This replaces the original speculative scoring/timed-gameplay ideas.

Every destination begins with exactly five individually authored clues, ordered from obscure to nearly unmistakable. Only clue one is initially visible. Players choose when to request the next clue; earlier clues remain accessible. Hints have no penalty. The fifth clue remains usable until the player chooses a selection or explicitly requests the answer.

Players rotate and zoom Claude's Earth, center a suspected location, and press keyboard X to finalize its coordinates. A selection at any clue level reveals the intended location and an educational explanation. After all five clues, the player may instead request the answer. Reveal may navigate Earth to the target. The explanation stays until an explicit continue action; accuracy is not graded.

Mysteries can concern settlements, countries, regions, natural or human-made landmarks, historical people, civilizations, events, discoveries, cultural achievements, or historical processes. Each V1 mystery has one meaningful point target. A person's target is not necessarily a birthplace. Regions, routes, multiple sites, timelines, historical borders, articles, filtering, and mystery chains remain future possibilities, not implemented features.

Long-term visuals include realistic terrain, imagery, oceans, atmosphere, and progressive detail. Physical Earth rendering remains separate from political borders, labels, and educational overlays. Accurate sources, uncertainty, cultural breadth, historical naming, and changing boundaries matter more than invented detail.

The existing launcher/title stays unchanged. The education foundation is headless and awaits review alongside Claude's independently developed Earth. See [EDUCATION_MODE.md](EDUCATION_MODE.md) for the content contract, session states, spoiler policy, and proposed integration boundary.
